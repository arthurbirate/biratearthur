🎯 Objective

The goal was to design and implement a multi-page HR analytics dashboard in Power BI, providing a holistic view of the workforce. It analyzed attrition, compensation, job levels, education levels, and satisfaction metrics, helping organizations drive workforce planning, retention strategies, and pay equity monitoring.

📊 Dataset

The structured HR dataset included:

  • Employee Info: IDs, roles, departments, job levels, education.
  • Compensation: salaries, pay bands, daily rates.
  • Attrition: active vs. exited employees.
  • Survey Data: employee satisfaction and engagement scores.

The data was cleaned and modeled into fact/dimension tables for smooth filtering by department, role, level, and education.

📈 Results

The dashboard delivered insights across four interactive pages:

  • HR Overview: Headcount, attrition rate, salary benchmarks, employee distribution.
  • Compensation: Salary distributions, pay equity by role, job level, and education.
  • Attrition: Identified high-turnover departments and roles.
  • Employee Insights: Drilldowns into demographics, job satisfaction, and engagement patterns.

📚 Key Learnings

  • Strengthened DAX skills (attrition rate, pay equity, tenure, satisfaction index).
  • Designed multi-page dashboards with executive KPIs + drilldowns.
  • Learned workforce insights: how job role, education, and level impact attrition/compensation.
  • Practiced dashboard storytelling to guide HR stakeholders.

🛠️ Technologies Used
