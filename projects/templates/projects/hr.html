Objective: The goal of this project was to design and implement a multi-page HR analytics dashboard in Microsoft Power BI, providing a holistic view of the workforce. The dashboard analyzed dimensions such as attrition, compensation, job levels, education levels, and satisfaction metrics, simulating how organizations can use data-driven insights for workforce planning, retention strategies, and pay equity monitoring.

Dataset: A structured HR dataset representing employee records was used, including:

  • Employee Information: IDs, job roles, departments, job levels, education levels.
  • Compensation Data: salaries, daily rates, pay bands.
  • Attrition Data: active vs. exited employees, lifecycle tracking.
  • Survey Metrics: employee satisfaction and engagement scores.

The dataset was cleaned and modeled into fact and dimension tables, enabling smooth filtering across dimensions like department, education level, job role, and job band.

Results: The dashboard delivered HR insights through four interactive pages:

  • HR Overview: Headcount, attrition rate, salary benchmarks, employee distribution by gender, department, and education.
  • Compensation Breakdown: Salary distributions, pay band differences, equity across roles, job levels, and education.
  • Attrition Insights: Highlighted departments and levels with highest turnover (common among early-career, lower-education employees).
  • Employee Insights Overview: Drilldowns into demographics, job roles, and satisfaction metrics to uncover engagement patterns.

These insights simulated how HR teams can identify retention risks, monitor pay equity, and strengthen leadership pipelines.

Key Learnings:
  • Improved DAX skills by creating measures for attrition rate, tenure, satisfaction index, and pay equity.
  • Learned to design multi-page dashboards balancing executive KPIs with detailed drilldowns for HR managers.
  • Understood workforce patterns: how job role, education, and level impact attrition and compensation.
  • Practiced dashboard storytelling to guide stakeholders from high-level KPIs to employee-level insights.

Technologies Used

  • Power BI
  • DAX
