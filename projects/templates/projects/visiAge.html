Objective: The goal of this project was to create a comprehensive AI-powered health monitoring system designed for elderly individuals. It focused on real-time detection of falls and coughing episodes — two critical indicators of health risks — with the aim of improving personal safety and enabling timely response.

Team Composition: A team of 6 members collaborated across different domains:

  • 2 members from App Development
  • 2 members from CCS Networking
  • 2 members from Artificial Intelligence (including myself)

My Contribution – Cough Detection Module: As part of the AI team, I was fully responsible for developing the cough detection system. My tasks included:

  • Collected real-world audio data (coughs and background noise) using iPhone and laptop microphones.
  • Processed audio signals and extracted features such as MFCCs (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients).
  • Fine-tuned a pre-trained XGBoost model to classify cough patterns.
  • Optimized the pipeline for real-time performance and integrated it with the main monitoring system.

Results: The module successfully detected cough episodes in real time with high precision, working seamlessly with consumer devices (iPhones, laptops). This demonstrated the system’s potential for cost-effective healthcare monitoring in elderly care environments.

Key Learnings:
  • Hands-on experience in real-time audio signal processing with consumer devices.
  • Application of XGBoost in healthcare-related detection tasks.
  • Practical knowledge of MFCC feature extraction for audio classification.
  • Strengthened interdisciplinary collaboration across AI, networking, and app development teams.
  • Building systems that balance accuracy with real-time performance for practical deployment.

Technologies Used
