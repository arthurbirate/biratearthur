Objective: The goal of this project was to develop a comprehensive health monitoring system for elderly individuals capable of detecting falls and coughing episodes in real time, thereby enhancing personal safety and response times.

Team Composition: A team of 6 members collaborated:

  • 2 from App Development
  • 2 from CCS Networking
  • 2 from Artificial Intelligence (including myself)

My Contribution – Cough Detection Module: As part of the AI team, I was fully responsible for developing the cough detection component:

  • Used iPhone and laptop microphones to collect real-world cough & background audio samples.
  • Applied signal processing techniques to extract features such as MFCCs.
  • Fine-tuned a pre-trained XGBoost model for recognizing cough patterns.
  • Ensured real-time performance via optimized preprocessing and integration with system interface.

Key Learnings:

  • Practical expertise in real-time audio analysis using consumer devices.
  • Experience applying XGBoost to healthcare-related problems.
  • Strengthened interdisciplinary collaboration across AI, networking, and app development teams.

Technologies Used

  • CoreAudio
