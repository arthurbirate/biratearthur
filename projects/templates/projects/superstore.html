Objective: The main goal of this project was to design a complete business intelligence solution using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, profit trends, and shipping performance. The dashboard was built to provide insights for different stakeholders — from executives to regional managers and supply chain teams — simulating real-world retail decision-making.

Dataset: The project used the well-known Superstore dataset, which contains several years of transactional retail data, including:

  • Customer orders with order dates, shipping dates, and priorities.
  • Product categories and subcategories.
  • Sales, discounts, quantities, and profit margins.
  • Shipping data with delivery modes and timelines.
  • Geographic breakdowns of regions, states, and cities across the U.S.

Process: - Cleaned and structured the dataset for analytics. - Created data models and relationships inside Power BI. - Designed dashboards tailored for different users: executives, sales managers, and supply chain teams. - Built DAX measures to calculate KPIs like total sales, profit margins, YoY growth, and shipping efficiency. - Integrated interactive visuals, drill-through reports, hierarchies, and filters for intuitive navigation.

Results: The final dashboard offered an end-to-end BI solution with clear visual storytelling and actionable insights:

  • Dynamic filtering by region, product category, and time period.
  • KPIs including Total Sales, Profit Margin, YoY Growth, and Average Shipping Cost.
  • Executive overview dashboards alongside drill-through operational analytics.
  • Improved decision-making for both strategic and day-to-day business use.
Key Learnings:
  • How to transform raw datasets into clear, actionable insights.
  • Best practices in BI dashboard design — visibility of KPIs upfront with drill-down flexibility.
  • Hands-on experience with DAX, hierarchies, bookmarks, and conditional formatting.
  • Improved storytelling through data visualization for both executives and operational users.
  • Strengthened ability to deliver engaging dashboards that support real-world decision-making.

Technologies Used

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Data Modeling
