Objective: The primary objective of this project was to design and implement a complete end-to-end business intelligence solution using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard analyzed sales, profit trends, and shipping performance using a fictional U.S. retail dataset, while simulating the real-world challenges of retail operations.

The solution was tailored for different stakeholders:

  • Executives: High-level KPIs and growth metrics.
  • Regional Sales Managers: Performance comparisons across states and product categories.
  • Supply Chain Teams: Evaluation of shipping modes and delivery efficiency.

By integrating interactive visuals, drill-through reports, and custom DAX measures, the dashboard became a self-service analytics tool empowering business users to explore data without SQL or technical intervention.

Dataset: The Superstore dataset was used, containing several years of transactional retail data. It included:

  • Customer Orders: order dates, shipping dates, and priorities.
  • Products: categories and subcategories.
  • Sales & Profit: discounts, quantities, and margins.
  • Shipping Data: delivery modes and timelines.
  • Geographic Coverage: U.S. regions, states, and cities.

This dataset provided a realistic scenario for analyzing how sales, profitability, and logistics interact across geographies and product categories.

Results: The final Power BI dashboard delivered a highly interactive and user-friendly solution for analyzing sales, profitability, and shipping at multiple levels.

  • Dynamic filtering by region, product category, and time period.
  • KPIs such as Total Sales, Profit Margin, Year-over-Year Growth, and Average Shipping Cost.
  • Drill-through reports for product-level and regional analysis.
  • Clear executive overview dashboards alongside operational analytics for managers and supply chain teams.

This combination of broad and detailed insights ensured that the solution supported decision-making at multiple levels.

Key Learnings:
  • Enhanced skills in transforming raw data into clear and impactful business insights.
  • Learned to structure dashboards so critical KPIs are visible upfront, with flexibility for deeper exploration.
  • Applied BI best practices such as hierarchies, bookmarks, tooltips, and conditional formatting.
  • Strengthened ability to deliver engaging, intuitive dashboards for both executives and operational users.
  • Improved storytelling skills through effective visualization and design principles.

Technologies Used

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Data Modeling
